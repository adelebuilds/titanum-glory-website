Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Titanum Glory Sdn. Bhd. handles personal information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, company, email address, country, selected area of interest and the information included in your message.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review and respond to enquiries, understand the support requested, communicate appropriate next steps and maintain the security and operation of the website.

Service providers and disclosure

Enquiry submissions may be processed by service providers used for website hosting and email delivery. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights and systems.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and apply appropriate measures intended to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your choices

You may ask about personal information submitted through the website or request an appropriate correction or deletion by contacting Titanum Glory through the enquiry form on the homepage.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
